# ⚡ TikTok Creator & Engagement Suite

An all-in-one local creator studio designed to legitimately maximize organic TikTok engagement through high-retention scripting, trend discovery, and automated 9:16 vertical short-clip reformatting with kinetic subtitles.

---

## 🚀 Features

### 1. 🔥 Breakout Trend Radar
* Tracks high-velocity viral topics, emerging sound formats, and retention patterns across major TikTok niches (Tech & AI, Personal Finance, Fitness, E-Commerce, Storytelling, and Psychology).
* Shows real-time velocity metrics (e.g. `🔥 3.8M Views/Day`, `+420% this week`).
* Recommends audio moods & BPM (e.g. `Phonk Velocity (138 BPM)`, `Suspense Drone`).
* **One-Click Send:** Click *"Generate Script From Trend"* to immediately import the concept into the Script Studio.

### 2. ✍️ AI Script & Hook Studio
* **Viral Hook Selector:** Generates 4 distinct, retention-engineered hooks for the first 2.5 seconds (Contrarian, Curiosity Gap, "Feels Illegal", and Brutal Truth) with retention probability ratings.
* **Teleprompter Script:** Structures full 30s/45s/60s scripts with color-coded stage cues (`[VISUAL]`, `[AUDIO]`, `[SPOKEN]`).
* **Engagement Magnet (CTA):** Craft comment-bait closing prompts designed to trigger intense debate in the comments (a primary algorithmic distribution signal).
* **TikTok SEO Generator:** Generates keyword-optimized captions and 5 targeted hashtags.
* **One-Click Action:** Export as TXT, copy to clipboard, or send straight to the Video Clipper.

### 3. 🎬 9:16 Shorts Clipper & Kinetic Subtitle Engine
* **Source Input:** Upload your own MP4/MOV footage or click *"Use Demo Video"* to test instantly.
* **Timeline Trimming:** Precise start/end time trimming with interactive preview.
* **9:16 Vertical Reframe:**
  * **Blurred Mirror Pad:** Keeps the full 16:9 widescreen footage in the center while dynamically blurring and mirroring the background to fill the 1080x1920 vertical canvas.
  * **Center Crop:** Zooms and crops directly into the center 1080x1920 canvas.
* **Kinetic Captions:**
  * Styles: **Hormozi** (Bold Yellow), **Beast** (High-contrast White/Red drop shadow), **Cyber** (Neon green/cyan), and **Minimal**.
  * Dynamic phrase pacing (3-4 words at a time).
  * Center eye-level or lower-third positioning.
* **Direct Export:** Fast server-side encoding producing ready-to-upload 1080x1920 H.264 MP4 videos.
